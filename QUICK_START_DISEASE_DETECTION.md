## ⚠️ Common Errors & Quick Fixes

| Error | Fix | Time |
|-------|-----|------|

| Still not working? | Hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R) | 1m |

## 📸 Best Practices for Photos

✅ **DO:**
- Natural daylight
- Clear, focused image
- Plant fills frame
- Show affected area
- High resolution

❌ **DON'T:**
- Blurry or out of focus
- Poor lighting/shadows
- Too much background
- Multiple plants
- File >5MB
